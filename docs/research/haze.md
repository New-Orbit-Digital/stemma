## 1. `haze`
- **Name:** Haze
- **Aliases:** Original Haze, O Haze, Haze Brothers Haze
- **Kind:** cultivar
- **Parents:** `mexican-sativa`, `colombian-gold`, `south-indian`, `thai`. This is the usual four-landrace account; see the facts.
- **Born:** 1969–1975. Display: "late 1960s–early 1970s"
- **Origin:**
  - Santa Cruz County, California, USA
  - US
  - 37.0, -122.0
- **Breeder:** Haze Brothers (R. Haze and J. Haze). Disputed.
- **Traditional label:** sativa
- **Growing:**
  - Historically outdoor or greenhouse.
  - Flowering is very long. Estimates range from 12 to 24 weeks: 12–16 (Soft Secrets), 16+ (Alchimia), about 14 (Humboldt), 20–24 (Leafly).
- **Sources:**
  - The origins of Haze | Alchimia | community | https://www.alchimiaweb.com/blogen/origins-haze/ | 2026-09-24
  - Haze (Unknown or Legendary) | SeedFinder | database | https://seedfinder.eu/en/strain-info/haze/unknown-or-legendary | 2026-09-24
  - The Strains That Shaped Cannabis Culture: Haze | Humboldt Seed Company | breeder | https://humboldtseedcompany.com/strains-that-shaped-cannabis-culture-haze/ | 2026-09-24
  - Sam the Skunkman: The Evolution of Hybrids | Sensi Seeds | breeder | https://sensiseeds.com/en/blog/sam-the-skunkman-the-evolution-of-hybrids/ | 2026-09-24
  - Haze Genetics | CannaGenie | community | https://www.cannagenie.org/cultivar-history/first-generation-hybrid-families/haze-genetics | 2026-09-24
  - Haze | Leafly | database | https://www.leafly.com/strains/haze | 2026-09-24
  - Where Did Haze Cannabis Come From? | Soft Secrets | community | https://softsecrets.com/en-US/article/haze-cannabis-history-pure-sativa-modern-hybrids | 2026-09-24
- **Facts:**
  - The usual account is that it was built over about three seasons. Mexican x Colombian came first. Those females were crossed to a South Indian male, then to a Thai male (Alchimia, Humboldt, Soft Secrets, CannaGenie).
  - Most accounts credit two growers known as R. and J. Haze, working around Santa Cruz in the late 1960s and early 1970s. Soft Secrets narrows it to Corralitos.
  - One history, citing Robert C. Clarke, calls them growers rather than breeders, because they never stabilized a seed line.
    - It says R. moved to Mexico around 1982 and J. stopped growing Haze around 1980.
    - SeedFinder also has R. retiring to Mexico around 1982.
  - Sam the Skunkman (David Watson) says the brothers gave him Haze and let him reproduce and sell it (Sensi blog). Humboldt says Watson was their neighbour and supplied some of the imported seed.
  - Nevil Schoenmakers (The Seed Bank) grew out Haze seed and kept two males, "A" and "C". A female "B" was dropped.
    - A is described as Colombian-leaning and spicy. C is described as Thai-leaning and earthy.
    - Those males sit behind later Haze hybrids: NL#5 x Haze, Neville's Haze, and Super Silver Haze.
  - Three phenotypes are attributed to the original growers: Purple, Silver-Blue, and Lime-Green Haze (Weedmaps, Soft Secrets).
  - CannaGenie says the name was settled around 1975.
  - **Accounts differ on parents.**
    - The four-landrace version is the most common: Alchimia, Humboldt, CannaGenie, Leafly.
    - SeedFinder describes a three-way of Colombian lines only.
    - Weedmaps drops the Indian parent.
  - **Accounts differ on the breeders.** CannaGenie says "Haze Brothers" was a brand name used by East Coast dealers. In its version, the actual growers were a surfer known as "G" and a Robert Lund, starting in 1969.
  - **Accounts differ on when Haze reached the Netherlands.** Sources give 1976 (Alchimia), 1982 (Cannapio), 1983 (CannaGenie), around 1984 (Soft Secrets), and 1985 (Sensi, which dates Sam's move to Amsterdam).
  - **Accounts differ on Nevil's seed source.** Some say he got it from Sam; others say from a New York collector holding 1969 stock. Sam disputed the second version.

- **Origin:**
  - Seattle area, Washington, USA
  - US
  - 47.6, -122.3
- **Breeder:** "The Indian", an anonymous Pacific Northwest grower. Brought to Europe by Nevil Schoenmakers / The Seed Bank.
- **Traditional label:** indica
- **Growing:** indoor; about 8 weeks
- **Sources:**
  - Nevil Schoenmakers | Wikipedia | database | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevil_Schoenmakers | 2026-09-24
  - Northern Lights #5 (The Seed Bank) | SeedFinder | database | https://seedfinder.eu/en/strain-info/northern-lights-nr5/the-seed-bank | 2026-09-24

  - 1989 High Times Cannabis Cup | Kind Green Buds | community | https://kindgreenbuds.com/culture/1989-high-times-cannabis-cup/ | 2026-09-24
- **Facts:**
  - The Northern Lights line was developed in the US Pacific Northwest and is mostly indica (Wikipedia).
  - Alchimia's account: a grower called "the Indian", on an island near Seattle, numbered eleven original plants. #5 and #1 stood out, and #5 is probably the only original female kept pure.
  - Nevil founded The Seed Bank in 1984, brought NL to the Netherlands around 1985, and sold the company to Sensi in 1991.
  - SeedFinder records a 1990 Cannabis Cup indica win for NL#5 (The Seed Bank). A Northern Lights entry, reportedly #1, won best indica in 1989.
  - **Accounts differ on lineage.**
    - Alchimia: pure Afghani at first, with Thai added later.
    - SeedFinder: speculatively "Purest Indica x Hawaiian", with breeder notes mentioning Thai x Indica backcrossed to Indica.
  - **Accounts differ on early history.** Some sources place the line in California before the Seattle grower had it.

- **Growing:** mainly indoor; 12–14 weeks (Green House) or 14–16 weeks (Mr. Nice)
- **Sources:**
  - Neville's Haze (Green House Seeds) | SeedFinder | database | https://seedfinder.eu/en/strain-info/nevilles-haze/green-house-seeds | 2026-09-24

  - Named in tribute to Nevil Schoenmakers.
